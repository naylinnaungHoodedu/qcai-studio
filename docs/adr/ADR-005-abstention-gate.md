# ADR-005: Abstention gate

**Status 2026-11-20:** the Context, Decision and Consequences sections below (the 2025 overlap gate) are superseded by "Decision 2026-11-20 (Phase 4: V1 deterministic validation)" at the end of this file. The Revision 2026-11-19 (Phase 3) section stays in force as V1.4. All three are left unchanged as the dated record of those decisions.

## Context

RevB treats weak-evidence abstention as a correct first-class outcome, not a generic error message.

## Decision

`ClaimGate` fails closed. It accepts only answer sentences with adequate overlap to at least one retrieved span; otherwise it emits a transparent `abstain` decision with reason and stores the failed claim bindings.

## Consequences

Coverage may decline, especially with provider-generated prose. This is deliberate: evidence digest/follow-up clarification is preferable to presenting an unsupported answer as grounded. Human evaluation remains the definitive faithfulness measurement.

## Revision 2026-11-19 (Phase 3)

The overlap `ClaimGate` of the decision above is withdrawn; its fail-closed rule stands: an answer that cannot be bound to evidence abstains, with a transparent `abstain` decision and its reason. This revision states what Phase 3 changed.
- **The overlap `ClaimGate` is removed** (plan 3.4: "overlap rebinding removed", R-13). So is its `evidence_digest` fallback. Nothing binds a sentence to whichever retrieved span shares its words.
- **`app/services/claim_gate.py` is now the segment subset checker**, rule `segment-exact-whitespace-v3` (plan Phase 3 E: "claim_gate becomes the subset checker"). Every factual segment of a rendered two-pass response must name a locked claim and carry that claim's text exactly, whitespace runs aside: the text is never NFKC-folded, so a rendering that turns "x²" into "x2" is an unmapped factual sentence (ADR-001 revision; spec conflict 32). Every citation must be one of that claim's locked spans. A rendering must also show at least one locked claim, and each claim it shows must carry at least one of its own spans; a renderer may leave a claim out, and the ledger records which claims and spans it showed. Its citation list must be exactly the spans its citation segments display, each once, in display order (`citation_list_mismatch`), so a record never names a span the learner was not shown and markers never skip a number; the ledger takes the shown spans from the segments themselves. Questions and connectives are typed by the renderer. A failed check makes the pipeline re-render, at most twice (plan 3.7); then it abstains with `subset_check_failed`.
- **Rule id.** `segment-exact-whitespace-v3` names the behaviour above; `package_check` fails when the package names another id. No ledger record outside development names any other subset-check id.
- **Abstention reasons** come from the two-pass pipeline vocabulary (`ABSTENTION_REASONS` in `tutor_pipeline/contracts.py`): the gate's retrieval reasons, plus `corpus_version_mismatch`, `no_quotable_evidence`, `no_whole_sentence`, `claim_plan_invalid`, `no_claims`, `no_bound_claims`, `subset_check_failed`, `render_failed` and `budget_exceeded`. `no_quotable_evidence` means the bundle held no quotable span (nothing approved under D-31, or the license gate excluded every result); `no_whole_sentence` means it held quotable spans, none of which holds a whole document sentence. `subset_check_failed` means every rendering that was checked was refused; `render_failed` means every render attempt raised instead, in the renderer or in the subset check on its output, and each such attempt logs `tutor.two_pass.render_failed`. The legacy contained path keeps `no_approved_span`.
- **Delivery.** Learners still receive only the D-19 contained answer. The two-pass pipeline runs in shadow until the Phase 4 exit.
- **Next.** The full rewrite of this ADR, with the V1 checks (V1.3-V1.9) and the sufficiency gate V1.5, is Phase 4 (plan Phase 4 F).

## Decision 2026-11-20 (Phase 4: V1 deterministic validation)

Every two-pass response is judged by nine synchronous checks before anything of it could be delivered (plan Phase 4 D; `apps/api/app/services/v1_checks/`; the V1 specification is section 4 of `docs/architecture/phase4-implementation-spec.md`). Thresholds, bands and the lexicon come only from configuration package v3 (`v1`, every value provisional under Q-22 and D-06: frozen before use, never reported as results).

| Check | Rule id | EHMF | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1.1 | `span-resolution-current-v1`: every cited span id resolves in the current corpus with the bundle's content hash | B | `hard_fail`: regenerate, at most twice, then abstain (`unresolvable_citation`) |
| V1.2 | `binding-polarity-v1`: the Phase 3 binding `resolver-bundle-sentence-v2`, plus `quantity-negation-polarity-v1`, which keeps every number and negation cue | A, B | the claim is dropped; no claim left abstains (`no_bound_claims`) |
| V1.3 | `entailment-interface-v1`: an entailment checker; D-21 is open, so the NullChecker (`none`) flags every claim | A, B, C | `flag` only, never hard; an unavailable or late checker abstains (`checker_unavailable`) |
| V1.4 | `segment-exact-whitespace-v3`: the Phase 3 subset check (the revision above), through the pipeline's one `check_subset` seam | D, E | `hard_fail`: re-render, at most twice, then abstain |
| V1.5 | `gate-decision-margin-v3`: the active retrieval gate's sufficiency, whose score is the top result's gate score (the score the gate compares with its floor: the 9-digit BM25F score under `bm25f-v1`, the P1 floor score under `p1-floor`, never the display or ranking score), with the package's X4 margin for that gate (an operator value under Q-22) | A | `abstain` below the floor; `mark` when that score is within the X4 margin above the floor, or not recorded (labelled `near_floor`, a V3 X4 candidate) |
| V1.6 | `keyed-answer-containment-v1` with `keyed-answer-socratic-omit-v1` | E | `hard_fail`: re-render with the socratic plan without the keyed claims, or abstain (`keyed_answer_leak`) |
| V1.7 | `conflict-version-polarity-v1`: version families, and aligned sentences of different items that differ in numbers or negation | D | a disclosure block (`disclose`) on the record, or the claims involved withheld; on a contained answer, the evidence panel's conflict labels under the policy `disclose` (and, on each read that shows the stored answer again, the labels the current package's rule gives its stored quotations), or the answer withheld when no panel is shown or the policy (`v1.v1_7.on_conflict`, an owner value under Q-22) is `withhold` |
| V1.8 | `misconception-all-terms-v1`: the versioned library (v0, empty while Q-14 is unavailable) | C | `flag`, withheld, queued for V3 |
| V1.9 | `schema-forbid-v1` and `fk-grade-segments-v1` | E | an invalid plan or envelope is hard; outside the readability band re-renders, then abstains (`readability_out_of_band`) |

- **HARD = final, by type.** A run ends in a `V1HardFail` (results only: no claim, no rendering, no consult path), a `V1Abstained`, or a `V1Passed`. Only a `V1Passed` can reach a V2 hook (`consult_v2`), and a `V1Passed` refuses to exist if its accepted candidate holds a hard fail; the one exemption is V1.2's drop of a proposal that never locked. A regeneration is a new candidate judged from the start; no vote, retry or later stage changes a hard fail (plan rule 1, invariant 3).
- **Modes (`V1_PIPELINE`).** `off` (development only) runs the Phase 3 composition alone; `shadow`, the default outside development, records the Phase 3 decision and V1's verification beside it; `enforce`, the development default, lets V1 decide the shadow record. The overlap gate no longer exists, so the "legacy gate" V1 shadows is the Phase 3 decision path (bind, lock, render, the subset check and its abstention) together with the legacy contained decision the learner received; every disagreement is logged (`tutor.v1.disagreement`) and counted. Enforce is refused outside development until the Phase 4 exit; off is refused outside development (plan 13.6).
- **The legacy guard has no flag.** Every contained answer delivered on `/assistant/chat`, `/tutor/turns` and `/qa/ask` passes V1.1 and V1.2 first: a quotation resolves, is verbatim in its span, and holds no number or negation cue that its span lacks. That is the claim-to-span rule, so a quotation never adds a number or a negation, but one that a span edge or the display truncation cuts can leave one out (DI-60). When no evidence panel is shown (`EVIDENCE_PANEL_ENABLED` off, its default outside development), or the package's conflict policy `v1.v1_7.on_conflict` is `withhold`, an answer whose quotations V1.7 finds in conflict is withheld too (AC-4-6: the disclosure is the panel's conflict labels, so without the panel, or under `withhold`, the answer is withheld). An answer delivered with the labels is stored, and every read that returns its quotations again labels them by the V1.7 rule of the package in force at read time: the tutor thread and the Q&A history, which the chat and the Q&A history render, and the owner's and the reviewer's ledger read (`GET /ledger/responses/{id}`, the legacy record's `conflicts`). Nothing stores the delivery's own labels, so a release whose V1.7 values differ (an owner value under Q-22) relabels every stored answer on its next read, and can drop a label shown at delivery (DI-68). Any failure withholds the whole answer as today's abstention and logs `tutor.v1.legacy_guard_withheld`. Without a package v1 block a governed build, and any build outside development whatever its governance setting, does not start; only a development build with governance off skips the polarity rule and V1.7.
- **D-20 interim policy, applied as its own text (PD-13).** Before Phase 5 every flagged claim (V1.3, V1.8, and the claims of a withheld V1.7 conflict) is withheld at every tier. The record names the replacement the learner got: the D-19 contained answer (`extractive_d19`), or its abstention (`abstention`); `unknown` where no legacy decision exists. A V1.3-flagged or V1.8-matched claim carries a V3 queue intent whatever the decision, delivered, withheld or abstained (under the NullChecker, every claim of a locked set), and a V1.5 mark adds the X4 intent to every claim. No "under review" delivery exists. With the NullChecker, nothing two-pass is deliverable.
- **V1.7 in production.** The shipped version family is the 2025/2026 lecture video (Q-10); video spans are never cited and the 2026 item is unbound, so in production a disclosure can come only from a number or negation disagreement between cited document spans.
- **Abstention reasons.** A V1-enforced record uses `ABSTENTION_REASONS` (above) plus `unresolvable_citation`, `checker_unavailable`, `keyed_answer_leak`, `readability_out_of_band` and `response_envelope_invalid`; a withheld response names `d20_interim_withhold` or `conflict_withheld`.
- **Residuals.** Plan M names two: "Residual: checker error, paraphrased leakage." Checker error: no checker is hosted (D-21). Its implementation-defined case here is a paraphrased contradiction between cited spans: only V1.3 could catch it, so AC-4-2 holds for it only while V1.3 withholds every claim as `none` (with a perfect-support checker the fixture report delivers the three such cases); it must be re-established when a checker is hosted (DI-64). Paraphrased leakage of a keyed answer: V1.6 matches the key's text, and keys under three tokens only as choice text. Also: the legacy quotations that a span edge or the display truncation cuts mid-sentence (DI-60), quiz feedback, which returns a lesson's keys to any caller that saves an attempt with placeholder answers (PD-21, DI-65), and a stored answer's V1.7 labels, which follow the package in force at read time, not the labels shown at delivery (DI-68).
- **Delivery.** Learners still receive only the D-19 contained answer. Two-pass delivery waits for the Phase 4 exit (DI-59); a `disclose` verdict's block is on the record and V1's accepted rendering holds no disclosure text, so the delivered path must show the block.
