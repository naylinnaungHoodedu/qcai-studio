# ADR-009: V3 audit and adjudication

## Context

Phase 6 of the upgrade plan (L1439-1498) builds V3, which "adjudicates escalations X1-X6 and a random 5% audit of deliveries [Evidence E-85]". Before it, no V3 queue, draw or gold store existed (R-22), roles were free strings with one guarded route, and nothing consumed the V3 intents that Phase 4 and Phase 5 records carry. The evidence is E-63 (one author, bus factor one), E-74 (Loop 4: adjudications and the 5% audit feed gold labels and thresholds), E-82 (two correction cycles), E-84 (arbitration rules; Dawid-Skene before 200 gold claims is a non-goal), E-85 (V3 human validation, 5% audit), E-86 (triggers X1-X6), E-95 (the private corpus boundary) and E-105 (the V-3 threshold). The risks are R-22 (audit bypass), R-35 (single maintainer: "the X6 auditor MUST NOT audit their own change"), R-40 (no SLOs) and R-49 (reviewer capacity). Holds H4 (D-18, "P6 pool; P11 start", release evidence "Capacity statement") and H10 (D-08, "P6 experts; P10, P12 cohorts") bear on the phase. D-25 (the critical-claim registry), D-18 (staffing), D-06 (operator values), D-08 and D-14 are open, and the freeze (D-05) holds.

## Decision 2026-11-22 (Phase 6: V3 audit and adjudication)

- **Triggers as pure functions** over the validated ledger records (`apps/api/app/services/v3/triggers.py`; trigger registry `x-triggers-v1`, `docs/architecture/v3-trigger-registry.md`; PD-32): X1 a claim the record marks critical that drew a family objection, at any tier (plan AC-5-4: "critical claims follow X1 from Phase 6"), withheld at tier 1 only (task 1: "withheld at tier 1"); X2 a claim escalated X2 with an objection of EHMF type A or B; X3 a learner flag (F_i = 0, never a verdict); X4 the V1.5 margin mark; X5 a model-version change or a CUSUM alarm (the statistic is Phase 9's, `cusum-alarm-v1` its interface); X6 the seeded audit draw. The critical-claim registry is versioned configuration, empty in every environment but development while D-25 is open (PD-33): X1 never fires outside development.
- **The sticky hold.** Under `MODEL_GATEWAY=on` a critical tier-1 claim that fails in any correction cycle is held for the rest of the response: never revised, pending in every later cycle even when a later tally passes (an objector that left the panel), and abstained with X2 (or withheld) at the end; it is never delivered. `holds.withheld_claim_ids` is the pure, mode-independent set of claims V3 withholds; nothing in Phase 6 releases one (release is Phase 7 change control, DI-93).
- **The queue** orders items by severity x exposure x (1 - K) in exact rationals, ties by enqueue time then item id (plan 6.3's EHMF weights, PD-34). **The draw** is a seeded, integer-only Bernoulli on (seed, response id): 1/20, or 1/5 inside an X5 window; each learner delivery is considered once, at its first scan; a same-seed replay draws the same ids (PD-35). The seed is public by design: the key is the response id the server mints at delivery, so knowing the seed lets no caller choose which deliveries are audited. A learner flag takes the scan's advisory lock on PostgreSQL, so an item's exposure is its record's however a flag and a scan interleave.
- **The workflow** is queued -> claimed (a 30-minute lease) -> adjudicated -> applied (PD-36). Every adjudication appends one row to an append-only, hash-chained gold store (`v3_gold_labels`; plan 7.4 AuditEvent) and changes no ledger row. 1/10 of items need an independent second review, a disagreeing pair a third reviewer. No reviewer reviews an item twice and no adjudicator applies it, judged on user-keyed round records (never on a keyed digest, which rotates with the guest secret). Reviewers see triggers, reasons, claim text and public, quotable span text only, blind to votes and their objection types, support share, severity, priority, finals and other rounds; the triggers and reasons say why an item is queued, so they disclose that an objection exists (X2's rule implies one of type A or B), never which family objected, a vote or the EHMF type it named; an item whose claims cannot all be shown is not reviewable. Applying exports the final label as recalibration input and releases nothing.
- **The reviewer gate** (`app.core.reviewer_access`; PD-38): a closed role set with `reviewer` through the authorization matrix; a guest or any other role gets 403 and an `authz.denied` event; outside development no caller is admitted while the reviewer pool (D-18) is unstaffed (an authenticated caller gets 403 and `authz.denied`; a caller without a credential gets 401 and no event, as demo auth is off there), and the ledger route's reviewer branch reads such a caller as an owner. Development reviewers are synthetic demo identities.
- **`V3_QUEUE`** is `off` outside development, `shadow` in development, and `on` is refused outside development before the Phase 6 exit (plan Phase 6 L). Plan L: "Disabling V3 MUST NOT release withheld items." The holds and the stop are computed from the records and the stored queue, so switching V3 off releases nothing; queue items cannot be deleted and move forward only (no UPDATE moves a state back, clears a hold, drops a trigger or re-points a final label). The downgrade refuses while the gold store, the queue, the flags or a CUSUM alarm hold rows, unless the operator exports first and passes `-x allow_v3_drop=true`.
- **The stop** (plan L, PD-37): when the oldest open item is older than the 72-hour queue-age SLO at every hourly sample of the last 48 hours, the V2 track runs tier 1 pre-verified-only (with no pre-verified source, every tier-1 claim is withheld); an unreadable queue is a stop. It is read through the pipeline's own database.
- **Residual error** is the Wilson 95% upper bound over X6-audited answer deliveries, per UTC month, report only (AC-6-5: 0 errors in 300 give 0.01264 < 0.013); the committed synthetic residual report (`docs/evaluation/phase6/v3_residual_report_v1.json`) is telemetry, not research results (plan H).
- **Configuration package v5** (5.0.0) carries every value, provisional (DI-98); v4 stays readable for one release (DI-100). One additive Alembic revision (`20261122_phase6_v3_audit`) adds eight tables.

## Consequences

Nothing learner-facing changes outside development: V3 is off there, the flag intake runs in development only, no reviewer is admitted, the critical-claim registry is empty, and the public payloads equal Phase 5's (the committed golden). In development the shadow pipeline records critical claims, the scan fills the queue, synthetic reviewers can adjudicate, and the V3 stop can limit the shadow's tier 1. The gold store holds production-plane labels about public-corpus spans only, the deployment form of HR-3 (plan L2593-2594), while plan L627-628 places "the V3 gold store" in the private research data plane: that store, for the study form, waits for D-14 and D-08 (DI-94). The labels stay out of the public repository, CI artifacts, logs and releases: the export refuses the repository checkout, the logs carry seq numbers and hashes only, and the API suite fails when any test writes into the checkout.

## Residuals

- Plan M: "[Risk R-22][Risk R-49][Risk R-35]; residual: reviewer error, bounded by double review."
- No reviewer is admitted outside development while D-18 is open (hold H4): no item is adjudicated there and every withheld item stays withheld (plan J; DI-91, DI-92).
- R-35 for authorship: no record names who authored the change an item audits (one author made every commit, E-63), and an erased reviewer's rounds leave only the gold rows' keyed digests, so that reviewer's independence on those items is no longer checkable (DI-99).
- The CUSUM statistic, "sustained", the stop-the-line and "promotions held" are Phase 9's (DI-96); Phase 6 tests the alarm interface with synthetic alarms only.
- Applying gold labels to thresholds, weights or the registry, and releasing any withheld item, is Phase 7 change control (DI-93). Dawid-Skene weights wait for 200 gold claims (DI-102).
- The delivered path's consumption of the holds and the stop, and a learner-facing "looks wrong" control with its privacy disclosure, wait for the delivered two-pass path (DI-97).

## Revision 2026-11-23 (Phase 9: CUSUM alarms, model changes and the ranking fallback)

- **CUSUM alarms.** The Phase 9 ranking scan raises its alarms (source `phase9-cusum`) through `record_cusum_alarm`, now
  with `db=` so the alarm row commits with the ranking events that name it; each opens the X5 window as before. A hold on
  promotions lasts until `v3.scan.window_remaining(db, v3_event_seq)` is 0 (open while V3 is off).
- **Model changes from the ranking.** `record_model_change` returns the inserted seq. The ranking scan records a candidate's
  version change under V3's lock with the key and version a record carries (`generator:<adapter_id>`,
  `<model_id>@<model_version>`; `generator-prompt:<id>` for a static generator's prompt hash) and inserts the
  `v3_model_versions` row, reusing V3's event when V3 already knows that version, so one change opens one window.
- **The queue reason.** Package v7's `v3.triggers.queue_reasons` adds `ranking_empty_eligible_set` (`QUEUE_REASONS_V7`), the
  ranking fallback's response item; it is not an X trigger, so `V3_COUNTED` is unchanged. Packages v5 and v6 keep the four
  Phase 6 reasons. `GoldLabel.reasons` takes the five; the schema packages v5 and v6 pin is reproduced by
  `GoldLabelV6Schema`, and package v7 pins `schemas/v3-gold-label.v1.r7.schema.json` (`v3_schema_models`).
- **The ranking reads F_i** (`v3.feedback.f_i`) for the quality term of a record the learner was shown; F never enters
  trust, the CUSUM or the selection.
